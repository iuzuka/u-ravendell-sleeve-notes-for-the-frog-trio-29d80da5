Entry for the [make.fun bounty `u-ravendell-sleeve-notes-for-the-frog-trio-29d80da5`](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ravendell-sleeve-notes-for-the-frog-trio-29d80da5).

An album title and back cover blurb for a jazz trio made entirely of frogs,
written as if by someone who has actually heard the trio play.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Output

Writes the title and blurb to `/output/entry.txt` (falls back to
`./output/entry.txt` if `/output` is not writable).

Final entry (363 characters):

> The Lily Pad Sessions
>
> Three frogs, one upright piano, and a room that smells of rain and old wood. Marsh leads with chords like low water over stone, Bull keeps the bass walking slow and sure, and Peep brushes the snare so soft you lean in to hear it. Recorded live in the back room at the Blue Heron, two sets, no overdubs — the night the pond learned to swing.
